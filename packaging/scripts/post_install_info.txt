GETTING STARTED
===============

1. Click "Launch SAM" below, or use the Start Menu shortcut.

2. SAM opens in your browser at: http://127.0.0.1:8765

3. If Ollama is not running, start it from your system tray or run:
     ollama serve

4. Your data is stored at: %APPDATA%\SAM
   This includes conversation history, task logs, and exports.
   It is NOT deleted when you uninstall SAM.

EMERGENCY STOP
==============
If SAM performs an unexpected action, click the red EMERGENCY STOP
button in the top-right corner of the interface, or press Ctrl+Shift+E.
